# Hamlet: Original Library Overview

**Work:** Hamlet  
**Author:** William Shakespeare  
**Library material type:** Original educational writing

## Overview

Hamlet follows Prince Hamlet after the death of his father, the former king of Denmark. When Hamlet learns that his father's death may have been caused by his uncle Claudius, who has taken the throne and married Hamlet's mother, the prince becomes determined to discover the truth.

The play develops through uncertainty, investigation, performance, revenge, and the consequences of delayed action. Hamlet repeatedly questions what he knows and how he should respond. This makes the tragedy not only a story about revenge, but also a study of doubt, moral conflict, appearance, and human judgment.

## Reading Focus

When studying the play, readers can pay attention to:

1. Hamlet's changing relationship with action and hesitation.
2. The contrast between appearance and reality.
3. The use of performance and deception.
4. The relationship between political corruption and personal grief.
5. The different forms of revenge represented by Hamlet, Laertes, and Fortinbras.
