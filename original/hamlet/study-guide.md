# Hamlet: Study Guide

## Questions for Students

1. Why does Hamlet hesitate before taking revenge?
2. How does disguise or performance affect the characters' understanding of one another?
3. How does the play connect private grief with political disorder?
4. Compare Hamlet's approach to revenge with Laertes' approach.
5. What role does death play in Hamlet's philosophical thinking?
6. How does uncertainty shape the audience's experience of the tragedy?

## Short Revision Prompt

Explain how Shakespeare uses uncertainty to develop the conflict in Hamlet. Support your answer with scenes or moments from the play, but write the analysis in your own words.

## Library Note

This guide is original material. It is intended for study and revision rather than as a replacement for reading the primary work.
